# Dstruct / public product vision

**Positioning:** An AI workspace to help web3 founders prepare for incorporation and coordinate legal work.

The public presentation follows a fictional team based in France and India, building on Ethereum with a multisig treasury and exploring a Delaware entity. No specific structure is recommended. Founder location, IP ownership, activity, governance, and token plans become questions for review.

## Scope

The current application supports synthetic cases, structured intake, linked official references, and brief exports. The animation illustrates future AI conversations, treasury context, collaboration, and document permissions. It does not imply that those features are implemented or that a blockchain deployment exists.

The public story concerns the founder experience and proposed outcomes. It does not publish private client material or a private advisory methodology.

## Web3 context

The product hypothesis is that advisers benefit from reviewing entity facts alongside governance and treasury context. Ethereum's [DAO introduction](https://ethereum.org/dao/) describes smart-contract governance and treasuries. [ERC-4824](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4824), currently in Review, explores relationships between onchain and offchain organization information. These are contextual references, not integrations implemented by Dstruct or legal authority for any incorporation choice.

## Presentation assets

The browser player and exported MP4 run for 136 seconds. English subtitles are burned into the video and also exported as an SRT file. No voice or music is included. The `story.js` transcript and deterministic `render.js` scene renderer make copy and timing editable.

[ETHGlobal Tokyo video instructions](https://ethglobal.com/events/tokyo2026/info/details) request a spoken demo and prohibit synthetic voiceovers. This silent website animation should not be represented as satisfying those instructions. The event permits submissions without a video.
